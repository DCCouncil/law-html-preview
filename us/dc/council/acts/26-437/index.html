D.C. Act 26-437. Extension of Substance Use and Targeted Outreach Grants Emergency Amendment Act of 2026.

AN ACT

To amend, on an emergency basis, the Department of Behavioral Health Establishment Act of 2013 to extend funding to all Fiscal Year 2026 grantees awarded to implement the Substance Use and Behavioral Health Services Targeted Outreach Grant Act of 2025 pilot program in order to continue services without disruption; and to amend the Opioid Abatement Fund Establishment Act of 2022 to authorize the Department of Behavioral Health to expend funds from the Opioid Abatement Fund to the grantees.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this act may be cited as the "Extension of Substance Use and Targeted Outreach Grants Emergency Amendment Act of 2026".

Sec. 2. Section 5120e of the Department of Behavioral Health Establishment Act of 2013, effective July 18, 2026 (D.C. Law 26-142; D.C. Official Code § 7-1143.02), is amended by adding a new subsection (g) to read as follows:

"(g)(1) Notwithstanding the requirements of this section, and subject to the availability of funds, the Department shall issue grants in Fiscal Year 2027 to the organizations that received grants in Fiscal Year 2026 pursuant to the Substance Use and Behavioral Health Services Targeted Outreach Grant Act of 2025, effective December 6, 2025 (D.C. Law 26-55; D.C. Official Code § 7-1141.01, note) ("grantees"), for the purpose of continuing to provide direct support, relationship development, and resource brokering to individuals in need of substance use and behavioral health services at:

"(A) The vicinity and related geography of the 600 block of T Street, NW in Wards 1 and 2;

"(B) The vicinity and related geography of the 1300-1700 blocks of North Capitol Street, NW, and 1600-1700 blocks of Lincoln, Road, NE in Ward 5;

"(C) The vicinity and related geography of the King Greenleaf Recreation Center, located at 201 N Street, SW in Ward 6; and

"(D) Ward 1 Community Navigator sites at the following locations:

"(i) Columbia Heights Civil Plaza;

"(ii) The intersection of Mount Pleasant Street, NW, and Kenyon Street, NW;

"(iii) Georgia Avenue, NW, between New Hampshire Avenue, NW, and Harvard Street, NW; and

"(iv) U Street, NW, between 14th Street, NW, and Georgia Avenue, NW.".

"(2) The Department may issue grant awards for services at additional locations.".

Sec. 3. Section 5012 of the Opioid Abatement Fund Establishment Act of 2022, effective September 21, 2022 (D.C. Law 24-167; D.C. Official Code § 7-3221), is amended by adding a new subsection (b-8) to read as follows:

"(b-8) In Fiscal Year 2027 and subject to funding available in the Fund, the Fund shall be used to reissue grants to all Fiscal Year 2026 grantee organizations awarded grants pursuant to the Substance Use and Behavioral Health Services Targeted Outreach Grant Act of 2025, effective December 6, 2025 (D.C. Law 26-55;D.C. Official Code § 7-1141.01, note), for the purposes described in section 5120e(g)(1) of the Department of Behavioral Health Establishment Act of 2013, effective July 18, 2026 (D.C. Law 26-142; D.C. Official Code § 7-1143.02(g)(1)).".

Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.

The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).

Sec. 5. Effective date.

This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than 90 days, as provided for emergency acts of the Council of the District of Columbia in section 412(a) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788; D.C. Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
