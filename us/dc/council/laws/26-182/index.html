D.C. Law 26-182. Glazier Licensing Standards and Certification Amendment Act of 2026.

AN ACT

To amend Chapter 28 of Title 47 of the District of Columbia Official Code to establish licensing standards and certification requirements for glazier and glazing contractors, and to add 2 members representing glaziers and glazing contractors to the Board of Industrial Trades.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this act may be cited as the "Glazier Licensing Standards and Certification Amendment Act of 2026".

Sec. 2. Subchapter I-B of Chapter 28 of Title 47 of the District of Columbia Official Code is amended as follows:

(a) The table of contents is amended by adding a new designation for part F-ii to read as follows:

"Part F. ii.

(b) Section 47-2853.04(a) is amended by adding new paragraphs (18A) and (18B) to read as follows:

"(18A) Glazier;

"(18B) Glazing contractor;".

(c) Section 47-2853.06(d) is amended as follows:

(1) Strike the phrase "consisting of 15 members," and insert the phrase "consisting of 17 members," in its place.

(2) Strike the phrase "and one shall be a consumer member" and insert the phrase "one shall be a glazier licensed in the District, one shall be a glazing contractor licensed in the District, and one shall be a consumer member" in its place.

(3) Strike the phrase "and elevator contractors." and insert the phrase "elevator contractors, glaziers, and glazing contractors." in its place.

(c) A new part F-ii is added to read as follows:

"Part F. ii. Glazier Licensing and Certification.

"Sec. 47-2853.100. Definitions.

" For the purposes of this part, the term:

"(1) "Board" means the Board of Industrial Trades.

"(2) "Glazier" means any person who erects, constructs, installs, alters, services, or repairs aluminum windows, curtain walls, interior glass partitions, glass handrails, aluminum entrances, skylights, and storefronts, and general architectural glass and metal installation.

"(3) "Glazing contractor" means a sole proprietor, firm, or corporation who, for compensation, engages in erecting, constructing, installing, altering, servicing, and repairing aluminum windows, curtain walls, interior glass partitions, glass handrails, aluminum entrances, skylights, and storefronts, and general architectural glass and metal installation.

"Sec. 47-2853.100a. Eligibility requirements.

"(a) The Board shall regulate and license glaziers and glazing contractors to ensure that persons engaged in those occupations conform to applicable national safety and performance standards.

"(b)(1) An applicant for licensure as a glazing contractor shall establish to the satisfaction of the Board that the applicant:

"(A) Has under their employ no less than 3 licensed or temporarily licensed glaziers who perform the work described by the applicant in the application;

"(B) Has complied with the bonding and insurance requirements established by rulemaking; and

"(C) Meets any other requirements established by the Mayor through rulemaking.

"(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) of this subsection, until rules are promulgated pursuant to paragraph (1)(C) of this subsection, the Board may issue a 2-year license to an applicant who:

"(A) Has under their employ no less than 3 licensed or temporarily licensed glaziers who perform the work described by the applicant in the application; and

"(B) Has complied with applicable bonding and insurance requirements established by the Board.

"(c)(1) An applicant for licensure as a glazier shall establish to the satisfaction of the Board that the applicant:

"(A) Has passed an examination on glazing that is acceptable to the Board;

"(B) Has a certificate of completion of an apprenticeship program for glaziers that is registered with the Bureau of Apprenticeship Training, U.S. Department of Labor, the District of Columbia Apprenticeship Council, or an equivalent state's apprenticeship council; or

"(C) Has comparable experience or a combination of education and experience that the Board deems equivalent to the above; and

"(D) Meets any other requirements established by the Mayor through rulemaking.

"(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) of this subsection, until rules are promulgated pursuant to paragraph (1)(D) of this subsection, the Board may issue a 2-year license to an applicant who:

"(A) Has a certificate of completion of an apprenticeship program for Glazier registered with the Bureau of Apprenticeship Training, U.S. Department of Labor, the District of Columbia Apprenticeship Council, or an equivalent state's apprenticeship council; or

"(B) Has passed an examination on glazing acceptable to the Board.

"Sec. 47-2853.100b. Representations and advertising.

"(a) Unless licensed in accordance with this part, no person shall use the words or terms "licensed glazier" or any words to imply that the person is licensed to perform the services of a glazier in the District.

"Sec. 47-2853.100c. Temporary license.

"(a) Notwithstanding § 47-2853.100a, in the event of emergency circumstances, the Board may, pursuant to rule, issue a temporary license for a period not to exceed 30 days.

"Sec. 47-2853.100d. Rules.

"(a)(1) No later than one year after the effective date of this part, the Mayor, pursuant to § 2-501 et seq., shall issue rules to implement the provisions of this part; provided, that both a glazier representative and glazing contractor representative have been appointed to the Board as required by § 47-2853.06(d).

"(2) If a glazier representative and glazing contractor representative are not appointed within 180 days after the effective date of this part, the Mayor shall begin the rulemaking process to implement the provisions of this part, to be complete within no later than one year after the effective date.".

Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.

The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).

Sec. 4. Effective date.

This act shall take effect after approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto) and a 30-day period of congressional review as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)).

Legislative History (LIMS)

Law 26-182, the “Glazier Licensing Standards and Certification Amendment Act of 2026,” was introduced in the Council and assigned Bill No. 26-422 which was referred to the Public Works and Operations. The bill was adopted on first and second readings on June 30, 2026, and July 14, 2026, respectively. After mayoral review, it was assigned Act No. 26-411 on Aug. 13, 2026, and transmitted to Congress for its review. D.C. Law 26-182 became effective Sept. 25, 2026.
